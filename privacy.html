Privacy Policy

Boostback · Effective November 7, 2026

Boostback is a game created by LD Clinton. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Boostback on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Information we collect

None. Boostback does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services, and the game makes no network requests while you play.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your company's progress and your settings on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Start the company over, at the bottom of the game's Hangar tab, or by deleting the app.

Ask the team

Questions you type to the team are answered on your device: by Apple's on-device model where your device supports it, and otherwise by answers built into the game. What you type is never sent anywhere and is not kept after you leave the game.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the Boostback support page.
