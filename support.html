Boostback Support

Help for Boostback on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, its system version, and the contract you were flying. Your company is saved on your device, so say what you were doing when it happened.

Three ways to fly

Fly on Easy
The computer flies. You launch, press each timing ring when the white ring meets the green circle, and steer the landings.
Fly on Normal
You steer, throttle and fly the landings yourself. On a landing, keep the throttle on the copper mark.
Watch it fly
Auto flies the whole mission for full pay but no stars. Tap Auto at any time to take over.

Controls on iPhone and iPad

The arrow buttons steer, the slider on the right sets the throttle, and you tap the ring or button in the middle when it appears. Docking and spacewalks add up and down arrows, and + and − to set your speed. The game is played in landscape.

Controls on Mac

Steer and throttle
The arrow keys, or W A S D. Left and right steer; up and down move the throttle.
Rings and buttons
Space.
Full throttle and cut
Z for full throttle, X to cut it. When docking, they speed up and slow down.
Problems in flight
1 or 2 picks a choice.
Everything else
T hands over to Auto and back, C changes the camera, P pauses, M toggles music, N toggles sound.

Controls on Apple TV

Rings and buttons
Click the Siri Remote.
Steer and throttle
Hold your thumb on the left or right of the remote's touch surface to steer, and at the top or bottom to move the throttle. A click on the edge of the remote gives a short push that way.
Auto, pause and camera
Play/Pause hands over to Auto and back. Back pauses the flight, and the pause menu has Auto, Camera, Music and Sound.
Docking
With the remote, put the target in the crosshair; your speed is set for you.
Game controllers
The left stick or D-pad steers, the right trigger means full throttle and the left trigger cuts it (when docking they speed up and slow down), A presses the ring, X hands over to Auto, Y changes the camera, and B pauses.
Menus
Move around with the remote and click to choose. Long text, like a briefing or a personnel file, scrolls a paragraph at a time as you move down.

Questions

There's no sound on my iPhone or iPad.
Turn off Silent Mode, using the switch or Action button or Control Center, and turn the volume up. Like most games, Boostback stays quiet when the device is on Silent. Also check that Music and Sound are on at the top of the main Farshore screen.
My iPad asks me to turn it sideways.
Boostback is played in landscape. A flight in progress pauses while the iPad is upright; turn it and press Resume.
A flight went wrong. Can I try again?
Yes. After a failure you can try that part again, let Auto fly it, or write it off and move on.
Normal is too hard.
Press Auto at any moment and the computer takes over. Press it again to take the controls back. Easy and Watch it fly are always there too.
How do I stop playing and come back later?
Your company is saved as you go. On iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, use Stop for now at the bottom of the Hangar tab; on Mac, Quit at the bottom of the Hangar tab, or Command-Q. On Apple TV you can also press Back on the main Farshore screen to go to the Home screen.
How do I start over?
At the bottom of the Hangar tab, choose Start the company over. It asks first, then erases everything and starts again from the introduction. It can't be undone.
Does "Ask the team" need the internet?
No. Replies are written on your device, by Apple's on-device model where your device supports it, and otherwise by the game's own answers.
Can I play in full screen on Mac?
Yes. Choose View › Enter Full Screen, or press Control Command F.
Does the game collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
